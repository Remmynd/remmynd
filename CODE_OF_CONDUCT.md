# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want remmynd to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, experience, identity or where they live.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers find their way.
- Accept the maintainers' decisions on scope, even when you disagree.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or using issues and pull requests to promote unrelated products.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainers at **connect@zoof04.com**. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block people who break these rules.
