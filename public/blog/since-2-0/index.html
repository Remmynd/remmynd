What's new since 2.0

Sign in if you want to, search with Ctrl or Cmd + K, a countdown that floats, a connector for AI assistants, a card for your own page, and smart paste. Still free, no account needed, no tracking.

Sign in, if you want to

You still don't need an account. If you want your reminders on every device, sign in with a passkey or a code we email you. Your reminders sync, and they can come by email too, an hour before and when it starts. Private links stay on the device they were made on. You can delete everything from your account at any time.

Search and commands

Press Ctrl K, or ⌘K on a Mac, or open Search and commands from the menu. Find any reminder or link, switch the theme or sound, or open a page. If what you type matches nothing, Enter makes it a new reminder.

A countdown above your windows

On a computer, a held reminder can float a small countdown above your other windows until it starts. It works in Chrome and Edge, and the button only shows where the browser can do it.

Kept this month

Coming up now shows how many of this month's reminders you marked done, once you've kept one. It only celebrates; it never shows a zero.

My plan

Saying when and where you'll do something makes it more likely you will; the research is on Why. On a held reminder, write it in one line, like "At my desk, 5 minutes early". Your alerts repeat it back, and so do the emails if you're signed in. It stays with the reminder, never in the link.

Share into remmynd

Once remmynd is installed on Android, it's in the share sheet. Share an invitation from any app and it lands in the bar, ready for Enter.

Smart paste

Paste a long email, or an image of a poster, into the bar. Where your browser has its own on-device AI, remmynd offers to read it. It runs on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. What it finds becomes one line in the bar, and nothing is made until you press Enter.

A connector for AI assistants

Assistants that take MCP connectors can add remmynd.space/mcp and make a reminder link from what you ask in your own words. It needs no account or key, and it stores nothing. See the docs.

The reminder card

Hosts can put a reminder on their own page with one line of HTML: the moment ring, the title, the time in each visitor's zone, and a Remind me button. It's on For hosts. The library and command line write it too, from 2.1.0.

What stays the same

remmynd is still free, still works without an account, and still doesn't track you. Every link made with 1.x or 2.0 still opens. The whole list, with the smaller fixes, is on the changelog.
