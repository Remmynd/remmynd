What's new in 2.2

Say the reminder out loud, a badge that counts down, a card for your Story, and a count of who's in, if the host wants one. Still free, no account needed, no tracking.

Talk

Tap the mic in the bar and say the reminder, like "Dentist Friday at 4 pm". Pause, and if you said a time, the link is made. Say only a day and the words wait in the bar until you add a time or press Enter. You can also say it the way people talk: "remind me to call the bank at quarter past 4 tomorrow" works. Where your browser can, it understands you on the device itself; the privacy page says what happens to the sound.

The live badge

A picture of your reminder that counts down, for a README, a forum or any page that takes an image. It ticks without any script, glows in the last hour, and says Live now when it starts. Make your link and tap The live badge under Share it, or see For hosts. There's a line for Discord too, which shows the time as a countdown in each reader's own time zone.

The reminder card counts down

The card you can put on your own page now counts down to the second in the last two days. Open it with ?screen=1 for a big-screen version with a QR code, for a stream or the last slide of a talk.

The Story card

Tap Story under Share it. remmynd copies your link and draws a picture of the reminder, on your device, for Instagram, WhatsApp Status or any Story. In Instagram, add the Link sticker and paste, and add the Countdown sticker if you like. No app can do those two steps for you, so the sheet shows how.

Add to story and Pass it on

Once you tap Remind me on someone's reminder, two buttons appear under your reminder stack. Add to story makes your own card, "I'm in.", and Pass it on sends the link to a friend.

The count, if the host wants it

Hosts can turn on "Show how many are in" when they make a link. From 10 people, the reminder, its card and its live badge show "128 in", and the ring grows outer rings at 10, 100 and 1,000. Everyone who taps Remind me sees their place, and the first ten see "First ten". If the host turns on the counter, we also keep one number for that reminder: how many people tapped Remind me. No names, no devices. It is deleted 30 days after the event.

Last call, and your year

For links you made, the reminder offers a Story card the day before and again in the last hour: "Last call", with the date and time. And on Coming up, tap "Kept 6 of 7 this month" to share your year as a card.

For developers

remmynd.space/new?q=Dentist+tomorrow+4pm opens the app with that line in the bar, and makes the link when it has a time in it. Version 2.2.0 of the npm package and command line can write the live badge, the Discord line and links with the count. See the docs.

What stays the same

remmynd is still free, still works without an account, and still doesn't track you. Every link made with 1.x, 2.0 or 2.1 still opens. The whole list is on the changelog.
