remmynd, in short.

What it is, facts you can check, and files you can use.

In one paragraph

remmynd makes reminders you share as a link. Write it in one line and share the link. Anyone who taps it is reminded in layers and followed through until it's done. No account needed. It is free, open source, and made by Zoof04 Inc.
