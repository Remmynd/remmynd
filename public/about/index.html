About

Who makes remmynd, and why.

remmynd is made by Zoof04 Inc. It turns any event into a link that reminds whoever taps it, in layers, and follows through until it's done. Free, no account needed, no tracking.

Why it exists

Plans rarely fall through because people don't care. They fall through because the reminder comes at the wrong moment, or never comes. remmynd puts the reminder where the plan is shared, in the link itself, so saving it takes one tap, and remmynd follows up when it's time. The research behind it is on Why.

What we hold to

  • The link is the product. Everything a reminder needs lives in its address, and old links keep working in every release.
  • Free, no account needed, no tracking. Signing in is optional, for syncing across devices and email reminders.
  • Honest buttons. Every button says exactly what it does, and none promises what the phone won't allow.
  • Small on purpose. No framework, a small Worker, and zero dependencies in the npm package.
  • Calm, plain language. Short sentences, and no numbers we haven't measured.
  • Open by default. MIT licensed, built in public, easy to self-host.

The full text, with what remmynd deliberately doesn't do, is in the principles.

Who's behind it

Zoof04 Inc. builds and maintains remmynd, and owns its name and logo. The code is MIT-licensed, so anyone can read it, run their own copy or build on it. The open numbers show what it's made of, and the blog says what changed and why.
