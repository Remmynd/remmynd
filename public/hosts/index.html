Put Remind me on anything.

A launch, a webinar, a product drop, a meetup. Your audience saves it in one tap and you never ask for an email address.

  • A badge for your site, post or email
  • A reminder card for your own page

Counts down

The live badge

A picture that ticks down to your event, for READMEs, forums and any page that takes an image. At the start it says Live now, and after the end, Ended. It follows each reader's light or dark setting; add ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to pin one, or ?size=mini for one line.

On your page

The reminder card

The ring, the title, a countdown and the time in each visitor's own time zone, with a Remind me button that opens the reminder. One line of HTML: put your shared link's code after /embed/. Add ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to pin a theme.

For a big screen, a stream or the last slide of a talk, open it with ?screen=1: the countdown in large type and a QR code of your link, with "Scan to get reminded" in place of a button. See the example on a screen.

<iframe src="https://remmynd.space/embed/your-link" title="Reminder: Demo day" width="440" height="112" style="width:100%;max-width:440px;border:0" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Private links can't be embedded: a card is drawn by our server, and a private link never reaches it.

Desk to phone

A QR code for slides and posters

This one opens an example reminder. Every link has its own: tap QR code once your link is made, or under More ways on any reminder, and download it as a PNG. It is drawn on your device.

Write links by hand

Links any AI can write.

A remmynd link needs no account and no API key. Any assistant that can write a web address can make a reminder, and assistants that take MCP connectors can add remmynd at remmynd.space/mcp.
