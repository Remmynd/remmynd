Questions

Is remmynd still free?

Yes. Making, sharing and saving reminders stays free, with no account and no tracking. Your reminder lives in the link and on your device. For phone alerts we keep only an anonymous ping time, deleted once it's sent.

What does Pro cost?

$7.99 a month, or $79.99 a year: ten months' price for twelve. Prices are in US dollars; tax, where it applies, is added at checkout.

How does the launch code work?

Code REMMYND50 takes 50% off the first year of the yearly plan, bought on this website, until 31 December 2026. After that year, Pro renews at $79.99 a year unless you cancel.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your account. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, and nothing more is billed.

I have the iPhone app.

Buy Pro in the app through Apple, or here on the website. Either way it belongs to your remmynd account, so it works everywhere you sign in, and Restore finds it from both sides.

remmynd is open source. Why pay?

remmynd is MIT-licensed. If you run your own copy, it has no limits at all. Pro pays for the hosted service at remmynd.space: the servers, phone alerts and emails, and the time to keep building.

Who handles the payment?

Polar, our merchant of record, takes the payment and handles tax. Your card details go to Polar and never reach remmynd.
