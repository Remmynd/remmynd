Privacy

Free, no account needed, no tracking.

Your reminder lives in the link and on your device. For phone alerts we keep only an anonymous ping time, deleted once it's sent. If you sign in, we keep your email address and your reminders so they sync across your devices and can be emailed to you. Private links stay on your device. Delete it all any time.

What we never do

  • No account needed, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • No cookies, except one that keeps you signed in if you choose to sign in.
  • The website loads nothing from other hosts. Fonts and code come from remmynd.space.
  • Request logs are off for the remmynd Worker, so it keeps no record of visits.

Your reminder lives in the link

A shared link (/r/…) passes through remmynd.space so chat apps can show its title and time. The Worker reads it only to fill those preview tags; it stores and logs nothing, and the page is never cached.

A private link (/#…) keeps everything after #, which browsers never send to a server. Turn on Private link when you make one.

Phone alerts

Alerts are optional. They turn on only when you allow notifications, after you tap Remind me.

  • The title, time and link stay on your device, in the browser's own storage.
  • The server stores three things per alert: a random id, your browser's anonymous push address, and when to ping it.
  • At that time it sends an empty push. Your device looks up what's due and shows it. The row is deleted as soon as it's sent.
  • Done, re-planning or dropping a reminder cancels the alerts it has left.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It keeps your reminders on all your devices and sends them by email. When you sign in, we keep:

  • Your email address, to send your sign-in codes and your reminder emails.
  • Your passkeys' public keys, if you add one. The private key never leaves your device.
  • One cookie that keeps you signed in for up to six months.
  • The reminders you hold and the links you make, so they sync. Private links are never synced; they stay on the device.
  • When each reminder email is due, deleted once it's sent.

Reminder and sign-in emails are delivered by Resend, which receives your address and the email to send it. Every reminder email has a link to stop them. In your account you can turn emails off, sign out, or delete your account, which deletes everything above at once.

On your device

Coming up and My links read the browser's storage on this device: the reminders you hold, their alerts, and the links you made. The theme and sound choices are kept there too, and so is your "My plan" line: it's saved with the reminder and never put in the link, so people you share the link with don't see it. Smart paste, where your browser has its own on-device AI, reads what you paste on this device; the text or image is never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it and stops the alerts.

Calendar files on iPhone

Some apps on iPhone, like Instagram and LinkedIn, ignore calendar files made inside the page. For them, /ics builds the file from the link in memory and keeps nothing.

Numbers on this site

The For hosts page shows GitHub stars and npm downloads. Our server fetches them from GitHub and npm itself, at most once an hour. Your browser only talks to remmynd.space.

Hosting

remmynd runs on Cloudflare, which carries every request to the site, as any host does. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Check it yourself

Everything on this page can be checked in the source code, which is MIT-licensed. Questions: open an issue on GitHub, or for anything private, see the security policy.
